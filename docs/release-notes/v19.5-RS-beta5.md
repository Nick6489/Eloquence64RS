# Eloquence64RS 19.5 Beta 5

This beta replaces the Presence contour checkbox with a Sound contour dropdown,
so you can choose the sound you prefer without losing your previous selection.

- At 11.025 kHz, choose Raw or Presence. The established Presence processing and
  22.05 kHz playback remain unchanged.
- At 16 kHz, choose Raw, Presence, or Smooth. Presence restores the exact Beta 2
  processing. Smooth preserves the exact Beta 4 processing, including its added
  high shelf. Both use the original released processing code.
- Remember a separate contour choice for each sample rate in each NVDA profile.
- Migrate the former checkbox automatically: unchecked becomes Raw; checked
  becomes Presence at 11.025 kHz and Smooth at 16 kHz.
- Fix a startup validation error found in the contour test package.
- Deliver speech-progress notifications with real audio chunks instead of
  zero-byte playback markers, and reduce native audio chunks to about 100 ms
  at the classic sample rate.
- Report pronunciation entries added, changed, or removed by dictionary updates;
  report identical downloads as already up to date.

Beta 4 remains available unchanged. This release preserves its 16 kHz sound
under Smooth and adds Beta 2's Presence as an alternative.
