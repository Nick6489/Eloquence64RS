# Eloquence64RS 19.5 Beta 5.1

Fixes Eloquence failing to load at NVDA startup when restoring a saved sound
contour. Settings restoration could try to refresh the contour dropdown before
NVDA had created its GUI, causing Eloquence to fail and NVDA to fall back to
another synthesizer.

The dropdown now refreshes only after the GUI exists. Saved contour selections
still apply during startup, and the dropdown continues to update when changing
sample rates in Speech settings.

Sound contours and other Beta 5 behavior are unchanged.
